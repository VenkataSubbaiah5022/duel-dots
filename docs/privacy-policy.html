DuelDots Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

DuelDots ("we", "our", or "the app") is a multiplayer board game. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Anonymous account ID — created automatically so you can play without signing up with email.
  • Gameplay data — room codes, match results, wins, losses, and scores.
  • Device analytics — app usage and crash reports via Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.

How We Use Information

  • Enable multiplayer matches and leaderboards
  • Track your game statistics (wins, losses, win rate)
  • Improve app stability and fix bugs

Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics). Firebase processes data according to Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Gameplay stats may appear on public leaderboards (username and win count only).

Children

DuelDots is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
